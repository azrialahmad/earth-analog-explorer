# Earth catalog selection-rationale and footprint audit

**Issue:** [#59](https://github.com/azrialahmad/earth-analog-explorer/issues/59)  
**Audited revision:** `05f8350b2181040d24ec6a3dc19efd843f14a40d` (`origin/main`, 2026-11-15)  
**Purpose:** trace why each current Earth entry is in the catalog, what evidence supports that rationale, and what is known about the analysed location. This is an evidence audit, not a score, site endorsement, field inspection, or global search.

## Executive summary

- The audited app catalog has **30 Earth entries: 12 initial reference entries and 18 scan-selected entries**. Counts were reconciled against `frontend/public/data/sites.json`; these are properties of this revision, not hard-coded scientific categories.
- The 12 initial entries are explicitly listed as proposed Earth test areas in `config/sites.json`. Its rationales are short descriptions, not citations. All 12 are marked `coordinates approximate` in the app; the manifest has `confirmed: false` for each.
- The 18 later entries were chosen by `pipeline/scan.py` from a limited set of DEM tiles and scan regions. The scan itself optimizes the existing terrain similarity and applies configured DEM/WorldCover/spacing/region-cap rules. This supports their description as **computational terrain/land-cover candidates under that procedure only**; it does not independently establish planetary-analogue relevance or field suitability.
- `config/scan_regions.json` explicitly says its 11 region rationales were chosen from general knowledge and “are not a sourced claim.” `docs/VALIDATION.md` says the scan is not global and that candidates were not checked on imagery or in the field. There is no checked-in `results/candidates.json` or per-window run record in this revision, so the exact historical decisions cannot be independently replayed from the committed artifacts. See #58 for scan provenance work.
- The `known_analog` field is provenance/presentation metadata, **not a quality tier**. Evidence for prior use varies by site and does not automatically support the particular 6 km square currently measured.
- The audit finds **no basis to claim that all 30 entries are independently established analog sites**. This does not show they are unsuitable; it shows that the app's current catalog rationale and footprint evidence are incomplete or uneven.
- No coordinates, flags, catalog entries, scores, or production data were changed.

## Method and status meanings

For each Earth entry, the audit compared the public app catalog against `config/sites.json`, `config/scan_regions.json`, `config/weights.yaml`, `pipeline/scan.py`, `docs/VALIDATION.md`, and `docs/REFERENCES.md`. The normal scored footprint is a **6 km × 6 km** window centred at the listed latitude/longitude, reprojected to a 30 m local metric grid; the app also records 3 km and 12 km windows. A regional source or field campaign is not by itself proof that this exact square falls within its tested field area.

Evidence statuses below mean:

- **Documented use:** a traceable source establishes that the named region was used for a relevant planetary/rover/field activity. It does not establish suitability for every task or validate this exact footprint.
- **Rationale only / not traced:** the repo states a reason, but this audit did not find a source supporting the specific selection and footprint claim.
- **Scan result only:** code and published run summary explain algorithmic selection; no independent suitability evidence or per-window replay record is checked in.
- **Footprint review needed:** approximate coordinates or broad site names make the correspondence between the published place and the analysed square unresolved. This is not a finding that the coordinate is wrong.
- **Potential discrepancy:** a recorded descriptor appears to warrant a human/source check; it is not a coordinate correction.

No numeric “validity” score is assigned. “No source found in this audit” means exactly that; it is not proof that no source exists.

## Cross-catalog findings and decisions needed

1. **Selection provenance is not symmetric.** Initial sites were manually proposed with short “why” text; new sites were algorithmically selected within bounded, agent-chosen regions. Both routes need scrutiny; neither route is a truth label.
2. **A scan score is circular evidence for its own selection objective.** The scan ranks with the existing terrain similarity, then publishes a shortlist. This establishes a computed match under those descriptors/config, not independent evidence that the terrain is relevant to a mission or test task.
3. **Regional activity evidence does not transfer automatically to a 6 km square.** This is particularly important where sources describe islands, valleys, craters or field campaigns, while app coordinates are approximate centres and often not a campaign-specific field site.
4. **Some source support is strong at region level, not footprint level.** Etna, Askja, Haughton, Dry Valleys, Yungay, Mauna Kea, Craters of the Moon, Black Point, Lanzarote and Svalbard have traceable regional research/training support. Bromo and Rinjani's current rationales remain geographic descriptions without a relevant selection source found in this audit.
5. **Potential review priorities:** Etna's recorded ~2600 m locality rationale versus 1947 m 6 km mean; the approximate coordinate/feature boundaries for every initial site; the use of Yungay reference provenance for scan candidates 81–90 km away; and exact geological-unit identity for every scan candidate.
6. **Do not infer a keep/remove decision from this report alone.** Decisions on coordinates, confirmation flags, wording, or catalog membership belong to the human owner. The report provides an evidence basis and states gaps without changing values.

## Limitations and follow-up

- This audit reviewed repository rationale/metadata and published web sources. It did not inspect high-resolution imagery, campaign GPS tracks, field logs, permits/access, or original scan rasters; no raw data were downloaded.
- Candidate selection cannot be replayed from this checkout: `results/candidates.json`, cached WorldCover responses, and the historical per-window exclusion/selection manifest are absent. Exact historical selection details remain an explicit #58 gap.
- The 6 km footprint is derived from current app/config semantics, not a newly generated GIS polygon. This audit did not regenerate terrain or verify raster-cell inclusion for each footprint.
- No attempt was made to find the globally best analog regions, establish independent task-specific relevance labels, or say whether an entry is suitable for field deployment.
- Any proposed move, new human confirmation, or catalog membership change requires an explicit owner decision and shared-contract review before generated values are recomputed.
